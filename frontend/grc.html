Merged GRC Module

CloudGuard evidence becomes audit-ready governance.

CloudGuard remains the scanning portal. The GRC platform is now included as a sibling module for compliance workflows, risk review, evidence approval, CERT-In MSME mapping, and reporting.

Open GRC Platform View Evidence Reason & Act
How the merged architecture works
1
Scanner jobs run separatelyAWS, GCP, Kubernetes, IaC, web, and demo labs produce findings outside the GRC app.
2
Connectors normalize evidenceCloudGuard and MCP outputs are converted into a common evidence shape.
3
GRC maps the resultsEvidence is tied to controls, risk priority, audit status, and remediation workflows.
4
Teams get one viewCompliance owners can see what failed, why it matters, and what should be fixed first.
Included Folder
The full GRC module lives in grc-platform/ with its own frontend, services, MCP evidence tools, docs, and test labs.
